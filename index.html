SYNOPSIS

The story centres on the comedic rivalry between two coworkers, Eve and Connor. In a dark twist, Eve’s “Employee of the month” streak is broken and the newbie, Connor, takes over. He becomes everything she cannot best. What ensues is a battle of constant sabotage, condescension and some questionable workplace antics. Workplace ties run deep, but rivalry runs deeper. Who will come out on top?

DIRECTOR'S STATEMENT

“Social Rejection and ostracism are fears of mine, and I wanted to convey that natural weak point in a fictional office workplace! While the topic itself is heavy, I still wanted it to be displayed in digestible medium.
